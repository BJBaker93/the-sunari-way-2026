28–30 September 2026

Know the house
Master the call
Earn the right to win

Great brands give you something to talk about. This is your chance to find the stories, flavours and ideas that make customers lean in.

We’ll get inside the Sunari house, discover what makes each brand distinct and taste the full vodka portfolio side by side. You’ll find the words that feel like yours—and bring them into the room as we put the five-step sales call into practice together.

We’ll get sharp on pricing, margin and the Ariane standard, then turn that confidence into a completed AVPAP plan for your account.

You’ll leave with Sunari Way accreditation, a practical Right to Win recommendation and a clear next move.

And then comes the challenge: what will you make happen in the next 90 days?
